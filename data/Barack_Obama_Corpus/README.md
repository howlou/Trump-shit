# The Barack Obama Corpus (2009–2016)

Annual XML files downloaded from the Lund University Humanities Lab Archive:
https://archive.humlab.lu.se/flat/islandora/object/lat:10050_00_0000_0000_0003_C53B_4

The archive describes the corpus as nearly 3,500 texts / 6,215,948 tokens, comprising official-capacity speeches by Barack Obama from January 2009 through January 2016. Its texts originate from the Obama White House Archives. Metadata includes genre, audience, date, location, and topic information.

Files: the_barack_obama_corpus_2009.xml through the_barack_obama_corpus_2016.xml.

Citation: Riesner, Katherina (2017). The Barack Obama Corpus [Data set]. Lund University Humanities Lab Archive. http://hdl.handle.net/10050/00-0000-0000-0003-C53B-4@view

The archive record does not state a reuse license for the underlying speech texts. Consult the archive and original source terms before redistributing or publishing corpus text.
